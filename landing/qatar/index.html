The problem

Growth breaks control

One site — the owner sees everything. Three sites — he controls only what he sees today.

Delays you learn about too late

Reports say "on schedule" until the week the deadline is missed.

Costs that drift

Purchases and spend are checked against the budget after the money is gone.

People you can't see

Strong specialists are rare. Without data you don't know who really delivers.

The solution

Four scales for every site

The AI agent works on the owner's side: it compares what happens on site with the plan and budget, and shows where to act.

01

Progress

Real readiness of each site in percent, against the schedule.

02

Quality

Work checked step by step, so issues surface before they are hidden.

03

Money

Spend and purchases against the budget — with a forecast forward.

04

People

Who delivers, who slows down, who deserves promotion.

How it works

The site brings the data. The system checks and looks ahead.

1

The site team reports

Foremen send updates, photos and purchases to the AI agent in a messenger — no new software to learn.

2

The agent checks

Every update is compared with the plan, the budget and the quality checklist.

3

The owner decides

Four scales per site and early alerts — where it's late, where money leaks, who needs attention.

For whom

Built for growing construction companies

  • Construction companies with 20–200 people
  • Several active sites at the same time
  • Owners and directors who can't be on every site
  • Developers who want transparency from contractors
